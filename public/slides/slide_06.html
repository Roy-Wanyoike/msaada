  • Core claim: "referral created" is not "help received" — typical tools close the record and go dark.
  • Msaada tracks the full lifecycle: CREATED, SENT, ACKNOWLEDGED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED — plus follow-ups PENDING, DONE or MISSED.
  • Outcome-tracking is what counties actually buy: visibility into results, not activity.
  • Volunteers get follow-up reminders instead of forgotten cases.
