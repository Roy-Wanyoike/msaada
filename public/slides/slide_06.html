Households are linked to encounters, encounters to triage records, records to referrals — the full identity chain is queryable for audit and supervision.

  • Core claim: "referral created" is not "help received" — typical tools close the record and go dark.
  • Msaada tracks the full 8-state lifecycle: created, sent, acknowledged, in_progress, then a terminal outcome — completed, declined, cancelled or expired. Terminal states are the dashed chips; completed is the filled one.
  • Every transition is validated by the referral API — a referral cannot silently vanish between states.
  • Outcome-tracking is what counties actually buy: visibility into results, not activity.
  • Volunteers get follow-up reminders after handover instead of forgotten cases — pending, done or missed.
  • Households → encounters → triage records → referrals: the identity chain is queryable for audit and supervision.
