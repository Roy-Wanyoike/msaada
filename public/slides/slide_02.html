The Problem

Kenya’s mental-health gap is not a treatment failure — it is a detection & routing failure.

90%+

of Kenyans who need mental-health support never receive it (Kenya Mental Health Policy 2015–2030)

90,000+

CHVs across all 47 counties, each covering 100–500 households — volunteers with paper notebooks and no routing tool

0

structured records of the earliest household signals: a mother who stopped sleeping, a child withdrawn from school, a father expressing hopelessness

By the time a signal reaches the health system it has usually escalated into an acute incident a facility must react to. The facilities exist. The CHVs exist. The policy framework exists. What is missing is the connective tissue.

location_on

Rural & informal-settlement households: Kibra, Mathare, Mukuru — where stigma blocks self-referral and the CHV is the only observer.

  • 90% treatment gap — the pipeline fails long before treatment ever starts.
  • 90,000+ CHVs are often the only observer inside the household — armed with paper notebooks, no routing tool.
  • The earliest signals — a sleepless mother, a withdrawn child, a hopeless father — are captured nowhere; zero structured records.
  • Reframe for judges: not a treatment failure, a detection & routing failure — the connective tissue is missing.
